Estimate Academic Examination Performance

This web application estimates a student's final examination score using a balanced weighted rule-based algorithm based on previous test scores, daily study hours, attendance percentage, and assignment completion rate.

Algorithm: Normalized Multi-Factor Linear Model Scale: 0 – 100 Marks Rule-Based Model (Educational Prototype)

  • Daily Study Hours (8h max ref): 20%
  • Classroom Attendance: 15%

Classification Criteria

  • 85.0 – 100.0: Excellent
  • 70.0 – 84.9: Very Good
  • 50.0 – 69.9: Good
  • 35.0 – 49.9: Needs Improvement
  • Below 35.0: At Risk

Formula & Normalization Logic

Predicted Marks = (0.50 × PreviousMarks) + (0.20 × NormalizedStudyHours) + (0.15 × Attendance) + (0.15 × AssignmentCompletion)

NormalizedStudyHours = min((StudyHours / 8.0) × 100, 100). Clamped strictly to [0, 100] scale.

Academic Purpose

Designed as an illustrative decision-support prototype for students and academic advisors to identify early performance trends and motivate balanced continuous engagement.
